# Traversability evidence is not a safety decision

Semantic classification describes visible appearance. Surface evidence derives image-space hypotheses. Safety requires future context, geometry, uncertainty and risk reasoning. Task010 contains no SAFE_PATH, steering, route, collision, TTC, alert or navigation decision.

Roads include shared spaces, road edges and crossings; their context is not inferred here. Sidewalk appearance does not establish access, continuity, clearance or absence of a drop. Grass, stairs and curbs must not inherit universal accessibility assumptions. Occluded ground is not freshly observed. Missing classes or objects mean unknown capability/coverage, not absence.

SAFE_PATH_VALIDATED: NO. PRODUCTION_SAFETY_VALIDATED: NO. Metric depth and physical motion remain unvalidated. Synthetic geometric tests do not change these statuses.
