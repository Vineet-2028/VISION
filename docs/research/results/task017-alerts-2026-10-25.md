## P. STALE-ALERT HANDLING

Realtime maps observation time plus processing delay onto the media timeline; offline uses source observation time and is visibly labeled. Expired text is not delivered. NO_PRESENTATION does not imply safety. Missing original source age remains unavailable, never silently zero.

## Q. DEDUPLICATION

Bounded ID ledger plus timestamp watermark reject repeated/old decisions even after eviction. Same decision is accepted at most once. Invalid batches cannot poison session state. Wrong-session input rejects until explicit reset; reset cancels pending state on seek/session change.

## R. PRESENTER QUEUE

One latest pending slot; default128 bounded history/ID entries, maximum512. New attention-selected output supersedes old pending text, without reranking risk. take(now_ns) rechecks expiry and consumes at most one item. No backlog drain; timing buffers also have fixed capacity.
