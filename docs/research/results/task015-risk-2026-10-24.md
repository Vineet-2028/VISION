## E. RISK LEVELS

## F. RISK VS EVIDENCE QUALITY

## G. RISK FACTORS

Current occupancy, usable predicted entry and possible envelope intersection support relevance. Receding/outside reduce it. Reasons preserve unavailable future, stale/predicted tracks, unverified semantics and unvalidated physical motion. Raw pixel distance and class do not receive arbitrary weights.

## H. RULE-BASED MODEL

Fresh observed occupancy -> HIGH (ELEVATED if all usable futures recede). Usable entry <=1s -> HIGH, later entry -> ELEVATED. Possible envelope entry -> ELEVATED. Fresh outside plus complete usable no-intersection forecasts -> LOW. Otherwise UNDETERMINED. Configurable thresholds are research choices.

## I. WEIGHTED MODEL / DECISION

Explicitly rejected for V1: no reviewed risk labels support extra weights, and correlated hypotheses must not become independent votes. No numeric risk score, factor-weight schema or speculative second engine. The protocol permits future replacements with identical output and regression gates.

## J. TTC / CPA GATING

DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY even for very small TTC/CPA. Unit/physical strings cannot authorize physical risk. Current engine does not emit PHYSICAL_METRIC_VALIDATED or SEVERE; no duplicate Guardian mathematics.

## K. STATIC OBSTACLE HANDLING

Fresh observed current occupancy remains HIGH without depth, semantics, future trajectory or TTC. Stationary/stable evidence is a reason where available, not a fabricated physical velocity.

## L. CROSSING INTERACTION HANDLING

Usable nominal entry and its time determine HIGH/ELEVATED. Unreliable trajectory or stale/source-mismatched forecast cannot support a future classification. Independent current occupancy remains available.

## M. PARALLEL / RECEDING HANDLING

Equivalent complete outside/no-intersection evidence ranks below crossing. Receding inside remains ELEVATED rather than becoming LOW. Research CPA alone cannot change classification.

## N. UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

Missing geometry or missing usable future outside yields UNDETERMINED. Empty sets retain capabilities and EMPTY_SET_NOT_SAFE. An unknown alternative prevents LOW; absence never supplies reducing evidence.

## O. STALE / PREDICTED EVIDENCE

Default 500ms source age/validity. Source identity times, wrapper joins, heading/relation sources and observed status are gated. Stale/predicted-only evidence cannot keep HIGH alive. Old output carries explicit expiry; disappeared IDs immediately leave history.
