## C. Optical flow architecture

Local single-owner frame-pair flow with one previous gray image. LK and DIS are interchangeable providers. Dense native-resolution vectors convert to bounded sparse correspondences for geometry. Optional masks and tracks consume public Sentinel values.

## D. Flow contracts
