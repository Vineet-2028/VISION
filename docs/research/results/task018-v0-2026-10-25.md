## AQ. TOP FIVE V0 RESCUE ACTIONS

### 1. VideoFileSource / realtime admission (MEDIUM)

Evidence: 18,001 frames decoded; 17,939 dropped; decode 249.641s + RGB conversion 110.999s

Suspected cause: Catch-up decodes/converts many frames that will be discarded, extending the next observation gap.

Experiment: Measure a bounded acquisition/discard prototype with identical source, model hashes and expiry; retain baseline.

Metrics: Decode+conversion wall time per source minute, source-to-world P95, update P95, drops; no timestamp regressions.

Success criterion: At least 50% reduction in catch-up time with correct PTS and no unbounded buffering; no safety claim.

### 2. Depth provider / pipeline scheduling (MEDIUM)

Evidence: Depth P95 3078.021ms; 60/62 updates first exceeded 500ms at synchronous provider 2.

Suspected cause: Serial depth blocks fresh world/reasoning publication; stale evidence propagates before attention.

Experiment: Bounded provider scheduling experiment with explicit old-depth validity and no expiry extension.

Metrics: Source-to-world/attention P95, expired candidates, stale dependency usage, memory/queue bounds.

Success criterion: World/attention P95 under the unchanged 500ms budget on the frozen source, without fabricating fresh depth.

### 3. Tracking and flow temporal continuity (MEDIUM)

Evidence: 61 tracker gap resets in 62 realtime updates; zero computed flow pairs; offline max track serial 5202.

Suspected cause: Approximately 9.8-second publication gaps repeatedly erase continuity; prototype IDs also fragment.

Experiment: After cadence fixes, compare continuity on reviewed identity clips with reset policy unchanged; separately test any proposed tracker change.

Metrics: Gap resets, flow pairs, identity switches/fragments, end-to-end source coverage.

Success criterion: Zero cadence-driven resets on this uninterrupted source and lower independently reviewed ID errors; never merely hide resets.

### 4. Interaction evaluation / reference evidence (LARGE)

Evidence: Historical forecast-entry recall 3/52=5.8%; new occupancy interaction availability is a different quantity.

Suspected cause: Point prediction, frozen corridor and weak identity references limit predictive coverage and interpretability.

Experiment: Adjudicate blind windows and actor/event timing, then compare frozen baseline against one bounded hypothesis.

Metrics: Independent event recall/precision, first-failure distribution, timing and ambiguous/censored counts.

Success criterion: A predefined independently labeled held-out set and a measured improvement with uncertainty reported; no training on the test set.

### 5. Attention episode policy (MEDIUM)

Evidence: 60 offline alerts (6/min); strict occupancy proxy reaches attention for only 1/166 upstream-qualified events.

Suspected cause: Global budget, novelty and fragmented episodes suppress many operational proxy events; many may be harmless.

Experiment: Review relevant vs irrelevant events, then bounded budget/episode ablation; do not simply announce every object.

Metrics: Reviewed relevant-event coverage versus alert burden, repeats/escalations and user preference.

Success criterion: Improved reviewed coverage at a predeclared tolerable interruption budget; unreviewed proxy gain alone is insufficient.

These are proposed experiments only. None was implemented in Task018.
