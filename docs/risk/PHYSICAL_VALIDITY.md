# Physical validity

IMPLEMENTED: assessments carry the supporting geometric tier or null when undetermined. Image occupancy remains IMAGE_GEOMETRIC. World geometry remains WORLD_RESEARCH_UNVALIDATED. A string claiming PHYSICAL_METRIC_VALIDATED is not an authorization: this engine declines that forecast tier until a future validation authority exists.

All TTC/CPA values are DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY, regardless of their numeric size. Relative depth, semantic classes and static-user research assumptions cannot promote physical risk. `physical_risk_validated` is always false. No SEVERE result or numerical risk probability is emitted.

UNVALIDATED: actual hazard recall, metric depth, physical ego/object/user motion, TTC, CPA, risk ground truth and production safety. Future validated stereo/ToF/LiDAR/VIO can strengthen existing source contracts; source eligibility and evidence authority must be reviewed before enabling physical policy. No equation or downstream contract rewrite is required.
